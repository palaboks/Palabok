Permission Overview

Primary Owner

Full system control, staff management, role configuration, settings, and audit access.

Co-owner

Moderation, staff oversight, and full audit-log visibility. Cannot remove, demote, ban, or override the Primary Owner.

Administrator

Assigned management and moderation permissions, limited by the Owner's configuration.

Moderator

Community moderation and handling of member reports.

Creator

Approved creator features and publishing permissions granted by management.

Member

Standard community access and member features.

Owner Protection Policy

1. The Primary Owner cannot be removed by the Co-owner.
2. The Co-owner cannot demote or ban the Primary Owner.
3. The Co-owner cannot modify the Owner's permissions.
4. Sensitive staff actions must be recorded in an audit log.
5. Only the verified Primary Owner can change protected ownership settings.
6. All these restrictions must be enforced by trusted backend code and Firebase Security Rules.

Audit Log Policy

The audit system should record who performed an action, what action was performed, the affected account, the timestamp, and the result. The Co-owner should have read access to the complete audit history. Ordinary staff should not be able to edit or delete logs.
