Submit an Appeal

Important: Be respectful and honest. Explain what happened, why you believe the moderation action should be reviewed, and provide any relevant evidence.

Appeal Guidelines

  • Submit only truthful and relevant information.
  • Do not threaten or harass moderators.
  • Include evidence when available.
  • Do not submit repeated appeals for the same case without new information.
  • Respect the final decision of the authorized community team.

Appeal Status

Real appeal tracking is not connected yet. Once a secure backend is configured, members will be able to check whether their appeal is pending, approved, or rejected.
