Moderation Action Types

Action Description Typical Use
Warning Formal notice to a member. Minor rule violation
Timeout Temporary restriction from participating. Repeated disruption
Temporary Ban Removal for a defined period. Serious or repeated violations
Permanent Ban Permanent removal from the community. Severe violations
Dismiss Report Close a report after review. Insufficient evidence or no violation

Moderation Guidelines

  • Review evidence: Check the relevant messages and context before acting.
  • Be fair: Apply the same community rules consistently.
  • Document actions: Record the reason, responsible moderator, and timestamp.
  • Protect privacy: Do not expose private reports or personal information publicly.
  • Use appropriate actions: Match the response to the severity of the violation.
  • Respect management boundaries: Moderators cannot override Owner-level permissions.
