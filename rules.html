Community Rules

Our goal is to maintain a safe, respectful, friendly, and welcoming environment for everyone.

Important: By participating in PALABOK COMMUNITY, members are expected to follow these guidelines. Community management may update the rules when necessary.
01

Respect Everyone

Treat all members with respect. Harassment, bullying, threats, discrimination, and personal attacks are not allowed.

02

No Spam or Flooding

Avoid repeated messages, excessive mentions, unwanted advertisements, malicious links, and disruptive content.

03

Keep Content Appropriate

Do not share illegal, harmful, exploitative, or otherwise prohibited content. Follow the platform's terms and applicable laws.

04

Protect Privacy

Never publish another person's private information, passwords, private conversations, or personal images without permission.

05

Follow Staff Instructions

Cooperate with authorized moderators. If you disagree with a moderation decision, use the appropriate appeal or support process.

06

No Impersonation

Do not pretend to be another member, staff member, creator, or administrator. Do not use fake authority to mislead others.

07

Respect Creator Rights

Only submit content that you own or are authorized to use. Give appropriate credit and do not steal or redistribute others' work without permission.

08

Use Support Channels Properly

Submit genuine reports and support tickets. Do not abuse the ticket system or submit intentionally false reports.

09

No Unauthorized Access

Do not attempt to bypass permissions, access private dashboards, exploit vulnerabilities, or interfere with community systems.

10

Follow Platform Policies

All members must comply with Discord's applicable policies and the terms of any platform used by the community.

11

Moderation and Consequences

Depending on the severity and frequency of a violation, actions may include:

  • Verbal reminder or warning
  • Formal warning
  • Temporary timeout or restriction
  • Temporary suspension or ban
  • Permanent removal for severe violations
12

Appeals and Fair Review

Members may request a review of moderation decisions through the official support process. Reviews should consider available evidence and relevant context.
